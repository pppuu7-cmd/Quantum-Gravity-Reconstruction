## PASS_SCOPED_KINEMATIC_EXTENSION

## PASS_SCOPED_CHANNEL_EXTENSION

If even the kinematic/direct-integral object cannot be source-identified consistently, or if the purported extension secretly depends on the undefined G8A `F_alpha`.
