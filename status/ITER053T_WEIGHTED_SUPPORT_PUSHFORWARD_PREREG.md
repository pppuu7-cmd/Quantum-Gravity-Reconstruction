## Dependency and exact diagnosis entering the gate

`B(u)=prod_i (1-(u_i/A)^2)^4`.

For an ordinary `CompactPerturbation pert`, `pert.base` is the unfactored polynomial tensor `p`, so the base weighted branch correctly contracts `H(u):p(u)` against the Gauss-Jacobi measure containing `B(u)`.

For the transformed C branch, however, Iter053R passed `pt=TransformPerturbation(pert,L)`. The wrapper has `pt.base=pert`, where `pert` is the full `CompactPerturbation`. Therefore the legacy expression

`pt.base.jets(u)[0]`

returns `B(u)p(u)`, after which Iter053R applies `L^T (...) L`. The Gauss-Jacobi weight supplies another `B(u)`. The resulting transformed legacy branch is therefore predicted to carry `B(u)^2` rather than the single physical `B(u)`.

Iter053S independently established pointwise H5 tensor-density covariance under the same two determinant-one shears, so the remaining question is whether a source-parameter-faithful single-weight pushforward restores the weighted representation.
