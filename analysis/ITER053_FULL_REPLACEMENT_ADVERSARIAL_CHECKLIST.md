Date: 2026-09-14
Status: outcome-independent review template only. Not a preregistration and not authorization to launch while active Iter053T companion gates are non-terminal.

## Gate-opening authority

A full corrected replacement is admissible only if both independently preregistered active Iter053T companion productions are terminal and mutually compatible with the corrected source-faithful weighted pushforward.

Before any replacement preregistration, a reviewer must verify:

- both terminal classifications under their own frozen contracts;
- no raw-lane evidence pooling between the two companion classifiers;
- no selection of the more favorable companion verdict;
- durable result notes, run ids, aggregate jobs, artifact ids and digests;
- recovery/state synchronized to both terminal outcomes.

If either companion is scientific FAIL or INVALID in a way that leaves the corrected object unresolved, the full replacement is not authorized.
