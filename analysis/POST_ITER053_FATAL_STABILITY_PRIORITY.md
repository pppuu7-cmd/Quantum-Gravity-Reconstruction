## Why a Weyl-active background changes the differential problem

For an action depending algebraically on the Riemann/Weyl tensor, the metric Euler-Lagrange tensor has the schematic form

`E_ab = algebraic(g,R,P) + nabla nabla P`,

where for the cubic Weyl term

`P = partial(Weyl^3)/partial Riemann ~ Weyl^2`.

Linearize around a background with nonzero Weyl tensor `Cbar`:

`delta P ~ Cbar * delta C + ...`,

and `delta C` contains second derivatives of the metric perturbation `h`.

The `nabla nabla delta P` sector therefore generically contributes fourth derivatives of `h` to the linearized corrected equations on a Weyl-active background.

This is qualitatively different from the flat background, where `Cbar=0` removes the linearized cubic-Weyl correction.

The exact coefficient and possible degeneracies are candidate-specific and must be derived rather than assumed. The important point is that the already-established flat TT spectrum does not settle the curved corrected principal symbol.
