## Why a curved multi-direction block is different

In more than one direction, the fine theory has ordered transports. For a loop the product

`W_loop = product_e L_e`

is a gauge-covariant holonomy and is generically nontrivial in a curved configuration.

A coarse description retaining only a local ten-component `G` does not, in general, determine the internal loop holonomy that was present in the fine block. Distinct fine configurations can agree on the chosen coarse local second moment while producing different ordered transports for later probes.

Therefore a `G`-only coarse map cannot be Markov/projectively closed on generic curved blocks unless a theorem proves that all relevant holonomy information is reconstructible from the retained coarse `G` data and their retained coarse derivatives.

No such theorem has been established.
