## Objective

After G1 fixed the full local two-derivative metric action, identify the first QGR structure that is **not** just another coefficient of classical GR.

The 24 equal `B4` ordering histories are already fixed by the microscopic/refinement construction. Their noncommuting directional transports provide a parameter-free source of finite-cell quantum coarse-graining corrections.

## Ordering expansion

Let `X_i` be the four anti-Hermitian directional transport generators on a sufficiently small cell and let

`U_pi = product_r exp(h X_(pi_r))`

for an ordering `pi in S4`.

Baker-Campbell-Hausdorff gives

`log U_pi = h sum_i X_i + delta_pi + O(h^3)`

with ordering-dependent second-order term

`delta_pi = (h^2/2) sum_(i<j) s_pi(ij) [X_i,X_j]`,

where `s_pi(ij)=+1` or `-1` according to the relative order of directions `i,j`.

The six commutators

`K_(ij)=[X_i,X_j]`

are the infinitesimal curvature/holonomy components seen by ordering changes.

## Exact 24-history combinatorics

Averaging uniformly over all 24 permutations gives

`<s_(ij)>=0`.

Therefore the order-`h^2` ordering displacement has zero mean. The exact covariance matrix

`C_(ij,kl)=<s_(ij)s_(kl)>`

has entries only in `{1, 1/3, -1/3, 0}` and has exact spectrum

- `1/3` with multiplicity `3`;
- `5/3` with multiplicity `3`.

No coefficient is fitted.

The two three-dimensional eigenspaces are the two `S4`-covariant sectors of the six antisymmetric pair/order components.

## Coarse traced channel

The normalized history instrument from Iter006 is

`K_pi = 24^(-1/2) exp(iS_pi/hbar) U_pi`.

After the history label is traced, the action phase cancels within each Kraus term:

`E(rho)=(1/24) sum_pi U_pi rho U_pi^dagger`.

Factor out the common unitary transport generated by `sum_i X_i`. Because `<delta_pi>=0`, the first **nonunitary** correction comes from the variance of `delta_pi`, not its mean.

To leading nontrivial order,

`E_phys(rho) = rho + (h^4/8) sum_(a,b) C_ab [K_a,[K_b,rho]] + O(h^5)`

in the interaction frame of the common unitary evolution, where `a,b` label the six unordered pairs.

Since the `K_a` are anti-Hermitian and `C` is positive definite, this is the leading term of a completely positive random-unitary/dephasing correction. It is curvature controlled: if all directional generators commute, the correction vanishes.

## Why the leading nonunitary order is h^4

- ordering-dependent BCH displacement starts at `h^2`;
- its exact 24-history mean is zero;
- channel decoherence depends on the variance of that displacement;
- therefore the first nonunitary term is quadratic in `h^2`, namely `h^4`.

This is distinct from ordinary discretization error in one selected path. It survives precisely because the model retains 24 microscopic order histories and then discards the history label at the coarse operational level.

## Fixed anisotropy in curvature-pair space

Diagonalizing the exact covariance shows a fixed relative strength

`(5/3)/(1/3)=5`

between the two three-dimensional `S4` sectors.

Thus, once the microscopic cell scale and the map from the six commutators to a physical curvature observable are fixed, the **relative tensor structure** of the leading history-ordering correction contains no additional tunable coefficient.

## Scientific significance

This is the first QGR result after all-orders local GR recovery that provides a candidate **beyond-classical-GR quantum correction** generated by the microscopic ordering structure itself.

It does not yet constitute an experimental prediction because the following remain open:

- identification of the physical refinement scale `h` in a normalized observable;
- continuum/RG running of the correction under many blocking levels;
- mapping the six commutator components to a specific laboratory/astrophysical observable;
- whether coherent access to the history register changes the effective correction before tracing;
- comparison with stochastic/decoherence effects in existing QG frameworks.
