`I3 = C_ab{}^{cd} C_cd{}^{ef} C_ef{}^{ab}`,

## Highest-derivative reduction

It is enough for differential-order counting to treat a generic quadratic Weyl monomial with the same index pattern, for example

`Q_acdb = C_ac{}^{ef} C_dbef`,

plus its algebraic Weyl/Riemann trace projections. Expanding the double divergence gives three types of terms:

1. `(nabla nabla C) * C`;
2. `(nabla C) * (nabla C)`;
3. `C * (nabla nabla C)`.

On every smooth four-dimensional Einstein background,

`R_ab = Lambda g_ab`,

one has the exact differential identity

`nabla^a C_abcd = 0`.

For the second-derivative terms generated by the double divergence, one derivative index is a divergence index of the differentiated Weyl tensor after using its algebraic symmetries. If the divergence derivative is already innermost, the term vanishes. If the two derivatives occur in the opposite order, commute them:

`nabla^c nabla^d C_ac.. = nabla^d nabla^c C_ac.. + [nabla^c,nabla^d] C_ac..`.

The first term vanishes by `nabla^c C_ac.. = 0`; the commutator is algebraic in curvature times Weyl. The same argument applies to the other factor. Covariantly constant metric/projector trace subtractions do not introduce an independent highest-derivative channel: when the complete projected P tensor is kept, their second-derivative pieces are part of the same divergence identity and cancel/recombine under the Weyl symmetries rather than defining an independent Hessian-of-scalar term.

Therefore the complete Einstein-shell double divergence can be rewritten schematically as

`nabla nabla P(C,C) = A[(nabla C)(nabla C)] + B[R*C*C]`,

with no surviving second covariant derivative of Weyl.

Since `C` contains two derivatives of the metric and `nabla C` contains three, the generic Einstein-shell Weyl3 source is at most third differential order in the known lower-order metric through this representation, rather than fourth order.

## Positive control

For a term linear in Weyl, the same mechanism reduces the double divergence immediately from `nabla nabla C` to curvature-commutator terms on an Einstein shell, reproducing the expected divergence-free-Weyl control.

## Negative control

The result does **not** set `nabla C = 0`. Generic Einstein metrics need not be locally symmetric. The surviving `(nabla C)^2` structures are real and prevent promotion to a purely algebraic curvature source.
