## A / CCRC — directed causal upgrade

### Construction
Use a finite directed causal chain of layers

`t0 -> t1 -> t2 -> t3`.

Causal direction is fundamental in the composition category: morphisms only compose forward between increasing layers. No backward morphism is introduced in this toy, so no directed causal loop can be generated by gluing.

Each causal link carries one of three relational labels. The label gluing kernel is the nontrivial `S3`-standard projector

`P = I - J/3`.

### Exact gluing

Because `P^2=P`, two successive fine causal links coarse-grain to the same kernel:

`P_coarse = P_fine P_fine = P`.

Three-link gluing is exactly associative:

`(PP)P = P(PP) = P`.

Thus directed composition does not reintroduce continuous coefficient freedom in this toy.

### Same-realization geometry survival

Use the same relational geometric operator as Kill Round 1,

`G = diag(-1,0,1)`.

The projected operator `PGP` is nonzero. Since the two-link coarse kernel is exactly the same `P`, the geometry mode survives the fine-to-coarse map **without switching realizations or retuning coefficients**.

### Verdicts

- directed causal composition: `PASS_SCOPED`;
- exact associativity: `PASS_SCOPED`;
- two-scale coarse consistency: `PASS_SCOPED`;
- rigidity under causal upgrade: `PASS_SCOPED`;
- nonzero geometry survival in same realization: `PASS_SCOPED`.

### Boundary
The toy uses a fundamental causal ordering of layers. It does not yet derive Lorentzian signature, 4D dimensionality, locality, Einstein dynamics, continuum measure, or an operational observable. The result shows only that the A-style rigidity mechanism survives the first causal/two-scale upgrade.

**Classification:** `PASS_SCOPED_DIRECTED_CAUSAL_TWO_SCALE_RIGIDITY`.

## B / CQCG — quantum-channel control

Consider the `SU(2)`-covariant qubit depolarizing channel

`D_p(rho) = p rho + (1-p) I/2`.

Its Choi eigenvalues are

`(1+3p)/4` and `(1-p)/4` with multiplicity three,

so complete positivity permits the continuous interval

`-1/3 <= p <= 1`.

Composition closes exactly:

`D_p o D_q = D_(pq)`.

Therefore complete positivity, trace preservation, rotational covariance and compositional closure do **not** determine a unique or discrete dynamics. Even imposing a continuous semigroup only yields `p(t)=exp(-gamma t)` and leaves a free rate `gamma` unless another principle fixes it.

This is a direct counterexample to the original proposed rigidity mechanism being sufficient by itself.

### Verdicts

- physical quantum channel: `PASS_SCOPED`;
- composition: `PASS_SCOPED`;
- rigidity: `FAIL_SCOPED` — a continuous admissible parameter remains;

Retained role: channel language may be useful later for normalized operational observables or coarse-graining maps, but it is not selected as the microscopic gravity dynamics.

## D / LCSD — spectral control

A minimal exact warning against spectrum-only geometry reconstruction is provided by two non-isomorphic five-vertex graphs:

1. the star `K_(1,4)`, degree sequence `(4,1,1,1,1)`;
2. the disjoint union `C4 + isolated vertex`, degree sequence `(2,2,2,2,0)`.

They are non-isomorphic because their degree sequences differ. Yet their adjacency spectra are exactly identical:

`{2,-2,0,0,0}`.

Reason:

- `K_(1,4)` has star spectrum `+-sqrt(4)=+-2` plus three zeros;
- `C4` has spectrum `{2,0,-2,0}`, and the isolated vertex contributes another zero.

Therefore spectral eigenvalues alone do not uniquely determine even a finite adjacency geometry.

### Scope guard
The D branch proposed more than a bare spectrum: an algebra/state plus a causal-spectral operator. The graph pair therefore does **not** refute every full algebraic-spectral reconstruction. It does show that any claim that geometry is fixed merely by spectral invariants is insufficient, and the branch still lacks a fixed dynamical spectral functional and a demonstrated Lorentzian composition law.

**Classification:** `PARTIAL / SPECTRUM_ONLY_GEOMETRY_NONUNIQUE / FULL_ALGEBRAIC_VERSION_NOT_KILLED`.

Retained role: spectral data may become a useful geometric observable or constraint inside another architecture, but D is not promoted as the lead microscopic dynamics.
